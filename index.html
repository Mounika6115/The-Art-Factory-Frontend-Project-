ABOUT US

Where Imagination Meets Creativity

Welcome to The Art Factory, a sanctuary of creativity and artistic expression nestled in the heart of Kanpur. Founded by the visionary Mr. Sumit K Kuthari, a seasoned artist with over 25 years of experience in the art field, and co-founded by the talented Himanshu Singh, a Master’s in Visual Arts and UGC NET qualified professional with 20 years of expertise, The Art Factory is more than just a studio—it is a celebration of art in all its forms.

Our journey began with a simple yet profound belief: art has the power to transform, inspire, and connect. At The Art Factory, we are dedicated to preserving traditional art forms while embracing modern techniques, creating a harmonious blend of the old and the new.
